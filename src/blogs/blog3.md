## Reading Code Is a Skill

Nobody tells you this but reading other people's code is just as important as writing your own. I spent my first year only writing, never studying. Big mistake.

Open source projects are free textbooks. Read them.

## Done Beats Perfect

I used to refactor before shipping. Constantly. The code was clean but nothing was ever finished. Shipping something imperfect teaches you more than polishing something nobody uses.

## Understanding Over Memorizing

I used to memorize syntax. Now I focus on understanding why something works. The syntax you can always look up. The mental model sticks.

## Burnout Is Real

Grinding 12 hours a day feels productive. It isn't sustainable. The best thing I did for my growth was take evenings off and actually rest.

## Ask Questions Sooner

I'd sit stuck for hours trying to figure things out alone because I didn't want to look dumb. Asking a question that saves 3 hours is always the right move.
